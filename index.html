MITOSIS STAGES (What to look for)

INTERPHASEIntact nucleus, diffuse chromatin, nucleolus visible.
PROPHASEChromatin condenses into visible chromosomes.
METAPHASEChromosomes align at the equatorial plate.
ANAPHASESister chromatids separate and move to opposite poles.
TELOPHASEChromosomes at poles; nuclei reform; cell plate begins.

5. FROM CELL DIVISION TO PLANT TISSUES

The onion root-tip cells in this lab divide rapidly in the apical meristem. After mitosis, daughter cells grow and differentiate into specialized plant tissues.

In a mature leaf, these include dermal tissue (including stomata for gas exchange), ground tissue, and vascular tissue, which transports water, minerals, and sugars.

1. Why would an onion root tip have a higher mitotic index than a mature leaf?
2. Which tissue system transports water and sugars through the plant?
